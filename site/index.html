Common Questions

Everything you might want to know before your first visit

Do you take walk-ins?

Scissors by Sandy is by appointment only. That's deliberate — one chair means your time is yours, with no waiting room and no rushing. Book online at scissorsbysandy.com or call (848) 230-5995.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

Life happens. If you can, please give me at least 24 hours’ notice, in Booksy or by call or text to (848) 230-5995. With one chair, that heads-up means I can offer your time to someone else. Thank you!

What's included in a hot towel shave?

The Royal includes warm towels, premium products and a traditional straight-razor shave with a beard trim. The Majesty adds facial steam, exfoliation and a customized mini facial to finish.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes. The Royal Roots is a wash and cut for our youngest gentlemen — patient, unhurried, and taken as seriously as any cut in the chair.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

The Shoppes at Old Bridge, 3813 US 9 North, Suite 101, Old Bridge, NJ 08857. There's free parking directly outside the suite.

Which areas do you serve?

Clients travel from across Middlesex and Monmouth Counties — Old Bridge, Matawan, Marlboro, Manalapan, Monroe Township, East Brunswick, Sayreville, Aberdeen and Parlin.

Do you offer gift certificates?

Yes. Call or text (848) 230-5995 and I’ll prepare a personalized certificate for whichever service you’d like to give — ready to print, or emailed to you to forward.
